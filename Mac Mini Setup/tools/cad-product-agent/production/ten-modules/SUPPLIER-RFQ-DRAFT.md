# Supplier RFQ — direct-to-customer modular frame fulfillment

Status: draft only; not sent.

Subject: Cut-to-length 10 Series extrusion and blind drop-ship capability

Hello,

I am developing a modular vehicle-camping product family using 1 × 1-inch
smooth T-slot extrusion compatible with 80/20 1010-S. Each customer module uses:

- 8 pieces cut to 18.000 inches
- 4 pieces cut to 22.000 inches
- deburred square ends suitable for bolt-together assembly

Initial demand is prototype quantity, followed by individual 12-piece frame
kits shipped directly to retail customers. At ten modules, the first family
quantity would be 80 pieces at 18 inches and 40 pieces at 22 inches.

Please answer the following in writing:

1. What standard and quoted cut-length tolerance applies at 18 and 22 inches?
2. Are ends deburred, cleaned and protected from cosmetic damage?
3. Can you ship each 12-piece kit directly to a different US residential address?
4. Can shipment be blind—our packing slip, with no supplier price/invoice or
   supplier marketing included?
5. Can our customer order number and kit SKU appear on labels and packing slips?
6. Can each kit be bagged or separated by cut length and protected from rubbing?
7. What fees, minimums and account requirements apply to direct fulfillment?
8. What are normal and peak lead times, and how are stockouts communicated?
9. Are substitutions ever made without written approval?
10. How are damage, missing-piece, wrong-length and address-correction claims handled?
11. Can you provide commercial resale terms and any MAP/territory restrictions?
12. Can matching 10 Series brackets, mounting hardware, panel gasket, tread strip,
    handles and feet be packed in the same customer shipment?

For the first sample quote, please price:

- EX-1010 or equivalent: 8 × 18.000 inches
- EX-1010 or equivalent: 4 × 22.000 inches
- 24 × 10 Series 2-hole gusseted inside corner brackets
- 48 × matching 1/4-20 button-head screw and economy T-nut assemblies
- 8 feet of 10 Series anti-skid tread strip

Please state material, alloy/temper, finish and component part numbers on the quote.

Thank you.

## Internal acceptance rule

No supplier becomes `DROP_SHIP_APPROVED` until all twelve questions have written
answers, one sample kit passes incoming inspection, and one deliberately damaged
or missing-item claim path has been confirmed with the supplier.
