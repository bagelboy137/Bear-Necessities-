## Commercial questions to send each supplier

1. Will you ship individual orders directly to our retail customer using our
   packing slip, with no supplier invoice or marketing material?
2. Is account-level blind shipping supported, and is there a per-order fee?
3. Can our SKU and customer PO appear on labels and packing documents?
4. What cut-length tolerance, deburring, cosmetic standard and lot traceability
   apply to extrusion?
5. What are normal and peak-season lead times, stock feeds and substitution rules?
6. Who owns damage claims, returns, warranty triage and address corrections?
7. Are MAP, reseller authorization, territorial or marketplace restrictions involved?

Until these answers are accepted in writing, the BOM's online links support
prototype purchasing only—not a proven customer drop-ship operation.
