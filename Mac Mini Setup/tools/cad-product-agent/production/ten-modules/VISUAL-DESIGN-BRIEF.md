# Visual completion brief — native Fusion rebuild

The reference image establishes a common silver 1-inch extrusion frame with dark
infill panels and recognizable functional equipment. Comparable products add the
details below. These are visual/functional precedents, not copied geometry.

## Family visual language

- Twelve separate silver extrusion members with visible dark slot lines.
- Dark charcoal side/back panels, black mounting hardware, rounded pull handles,
  visible fasteners and a small front module ID plate.
- Functional equipment must be recognizable from an isometric screenshot without
  reading the filename.
- Keep the 24W × 20D × 18H outer frame and 22W × 18D × 16H clear region.
- Each model is a native multi-component Fusion document, not one fused body.

## Comparable-product cues carried forward

- Overland Kitchen EX1: enclosing fridge cabinet, dual lock-in/out slides,
  ventilation slots, drain/service access and vehicle strap/bolt patterns.
- Goose Gear CampKitchen: independently moving stove/fridge slides.
- OVS Mid-Size Kitchen: separate cooking, prep and sink layers; removable basin,
  wind guard, tie-down straps and anchor points.
- Lone Peak / AT Overland drawers: divided drawers, textured dark finish,
  full-extension guides and locking compression latches.
- MODULR/OVS utility systems: precision-cut MOLLE pattern and adjustable shelf.
- Tailgate Gear kitchen: aluminum-profile frame, Dibond panels, foldable faucet,
  water pump, collapsible basin, lockable sliders and gas restraint.

## Visual QA

Every module must have its own 1200×900 isometric PNG. Review at least three
times: geometry/detail checklist, comparable-product cue checklist, and family
consistency/legibility. A screenshot consisting mainly of anonymous cuboids fails.
