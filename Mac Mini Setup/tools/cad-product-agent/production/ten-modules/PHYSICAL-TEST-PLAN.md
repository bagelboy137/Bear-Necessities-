# Prototype physical acceptance plan

This is a verification plan, not a structural certification. Load magnitudes
remain blank until Conor approves intended payload and vehicle deceleration cases.

## Stage 0 — incoming inspection

Record supplier, PO, lot/date, packaging condition and calibrated measurement tool.

- Measure all 8 × 18-inch and 4 × 22-inch pieces at three temperatures/locations.
- Record length, squareness, burrs, twist, bow, anodize scratches and slot damage.
- Verify profile, alloy/finish declaration and every hardware part number/count.
- Reject mixed unlabeled hardware, unapproved substitutions and sharp edges.
- Archive photos and measurements under the job revision.

Acceptance values requiring approval:

- Cut-length tolerance: supplier quote or tighter drawing requirement: ______
- End squareness: ______
- Maximum cosmetic defect: ______
- Maximum bow/twist: ______

## Stage 1 — common empty frame

- Assemble to documented sequence and torque; record tool and final torque.
- Measure outside W/D/H, both diagonals on all six faces, and empty mass.
- Confirm every module remains within 24 × 20 × 18 inches including handles/feet.
- Carry through handles for 100 cycles at approved test load.
- Disassemble/reassemble three times and remeasure geometry and torque retention.
- Inspect for slot damage, bracket slip, fastener loosening and permanent set.

## Stage 2 — static proof load

Required inputs before test: rated payload `P`, vertical dynamic factor `Dv`,
longitudinal factor `Dl`, lateral factor `Dt`, and target safety factor `SF`.

- Uniform floor load: `P × Dv × SF` for the approved duration.
- Handle/lift test: frame plus `P`, independently at each intended lift orientation.
- Forward, rearward and lateral restraint proof loads: `P × D × SF`.
- Record displacement under load, residual displacement, joint slip and damage.
- Stop immediately for audible cracking, progressive slip, sharp deformation or
  unsafe fixture behavior.

The fixture and load application must be reviewed before testing. Never place a
person in the load path.

## Stage 3 — transport vibration and retention

- Install in the representative vehicle using the production-intent anchor system.
- Instrument/mark every joint and fastener; use witness marks.
- Run the approved road/vibration profile with representative payload and center
  of gravity, then repeat dimensional/torque inspection.
- Verify locking pins cannot release, module cannot migrate, and adjacent modules
  cannot collide or abrade wiring/plumbing.

## Stage 4 — module-specific tests

| Module | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| BN-M01 Cook | Transported propane separation; outdoor deployment; hot-surface clearance; stability; extinguishing/emergency procedure |
| BN-M02 Pantry | Actual container fit and lid access; packaged-food statement; latch retention; spill cleanup |
| BN-M03 Fridge | Authorized in-stock unit; slide-out/lid access; compressor ventilation; fused 12V wiring; incline/vibration behavior |
| BN-M04 Sink | Exact sink cutout; potable components; leak/pressure/drain test; grey-water containment; freeze drainage |
| BN-M05 Water | Full 7-gallon mass restraint; spill tray; pump current/fuse; potable sanitation; leak and slosh test |
| BN-M06 Utility | Actual box fit; declared payload; recovery-item restraint; sharp/heavy-object containment |
| BN-M07 Power | Manufacturer clearances; port access; thermal run at maximum intended load/charge; cable strain/fuse/fire labeling |
| BN-M08 Hot water | Transport-only orientation; outdoor-only interlock/label; wet-hose drying; propane separation; leak test |
| BN-M09 Office | Pelican 1485 configuration fit; retention; worktop proof load; hinge/cable stops; electronics ventilation |
| BN-M10 Soft goods | Selected chair/table fit; net/strap ratings; compression retention; no loose-projectile condition |

## Stage 5 — usability and fulfillment

- Two representative users install, lock, unlock, remove and deploy every module.
- Record pinch points, lift posture, time, instructions failures and loose parts.
- Perform one complete supplier-to-customer shipment and inspect damage/missing items.
- Confirm serial/revision traceability and that the customer can identify every part.

## Pass record

Every test record requires job ID/revision, date, operator, equipment/calibration,
input values, raw measurements, photos/video links, deviations, pass/fail and
reviewer signature. A summary without raw evidence is not a release record.
