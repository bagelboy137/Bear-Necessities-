# Part C-01: Module Anchor Plate

**Purpose:** bolts into the T-slot on the underside of a module's base rail and presents a pin hole so the module can be locked to the vehicle floor bracket (C-02).
**Process:** laser cut from 1/4" (0.250") 5052-H32 aluminium sheet. Flat. No bends, no machining.
**Qty:** 2 per module (one each side).

## Geometry
- Overall plate: **4.000" long × 1.500" wide × 0.250" thick**
- **2 × mounting holes**, Ø0.281" (clearance for 1/4-20), on the plate centreline, spaced **2.000"** apart, symmetric about the plate centre. These bolt into the 1010 T-slot with standard T-nuts.
- **1 × pin hole**, Ø0.266" (clearance for a 1/4" quick-release pin), on the centreline, **1.500"** from the plate centre toward one end.
- **Corner radius 0.250"** on all four corners.

## Interfaces (must be exact)
| Feature | Dimension | Tolerance | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mounting hole spacing | 2.000" | ±0.010" | must land on T-slot centres |
| Pin hole diameter | 0.266" | +0.005/-0.000 | must accept a 1/4" pin freely |
| Pin hole position from plate centre | 1.500" | ±0.010" | must align with C-02 |
| Thickness | 0.250" | sheet tolerance | shear strength |

## Explicitly free
Outline styling beyond the stated radius, any lightening cutouts, edge chamfer.

## Deliverable
STEP (3D) + **DXF of the flat profile** — the DXF is what a laser shop quotes from.
